`msaa::depth_test_holds_on_a_multisampled_target` intermittently reads an entirely opaque-black resolved row on the Intel i686 CI leg. This remains open after #522; a complete fix has not been established.

The original failures were on main runs [34048431559](https://github.com/athei/mtld3d/actions/runs/34048431559) at 87a43bb and [34101662844](https://github.com/athei/mtld3d/actions/runs/34101662844) at 5d598d8. #522 added a paravirtual-only retirement wait before copying out of a multisample resolve target, and changed the assertion to print the row. It did not close this issue.

Two later main attempts reproduced the assertion after that mitigation:

- [34166547160, attempt 1, Intel i686 job 101880258294](https://github.com/athei/mtld3d/actions/runs/34166547160/job/101880258294), at 485f3d5254be6d361aee1d90f280affbd69f2556 on September 7. `e2e-6578` printed an all-`FF000000` row and the leg ended with 523 passed, 1 failed. Attempt 2 passed the target on the unchanged commit. The currently downloadable layer-log artifact contains only the retry's files, so the original process's command-buffer and driver state cannot be reconstructed from that artifact.
- [34479501158, attempt 1, Intel i686 job 102880263379](https://github.com/athei/mtld3d/actions/runs/34479501158/job/102880263379), at 45bad5d82583d101aef0be1d488f5e0b31a92b73 on September 10. `e2e-10634.stderr` records the same assertion at `msaa.rs:536`, with all 640 pixels `FF000000`. The leg ended with 555 passed, 3 failed; its two shader-cache failures occurred in later processes and are separate findings.

The September 10 layer log has no command-buffer failure, failed readback, or valid-size backbuffer allocation rejection. Its invalid 65535x65535 request is an intentional negative test. An optional sRGB-view refusal occurred earlier in the process. The retained system log records three `AppleParavirtGPUMetal` `Failed to add child resource: e00002bd` errors for PID 10634 at 13:09:44.605, .769 and .923 UTC, beside `addObject: Object already exists` and `resource already has a map` errors. The MSAA test creates its device at 13:09:52. There is no GPU-hang signature in these retained logs. The system errors carry no resource identity, so they cannot be tied to the MSAA target. They resemble the driver-error family investigated in #477, but a shared cause is not established; #477's valid-size `newTextureWithDescriptor` allocation failure did not occur here.

Source review at cdf836dbe97d9db46d3ed33a8d7cda3a7c37fc66 found no demonstrable ordering defect in this test's path. The last MSAA pass resolves into the single-sample twin. `emit_stretch_rect_blit` calls the paravirtual retirement wait for a source with an MSAA companion, and that wait drains prior submissions before waiting for the preceding sequence. Managed readback encodes `synchronizeResource`, waits for completion, and rejects unsuccessful completion. This establishes the intended ordering in source, not successful execution of the failing producer. The detailed command-buffer target was disabled in the retained failure, so its producer/resolve/copy identities and completion states remain unobserved.

The next discriminating evidence is a recurrence on the affected device with the existing `mtld3d::unix::command=debug` target enabled for this test. #579, #608 and #611 already provide command-buffer status/error, render-pass attachments, copy endpoints and readback records. Follow those records within one process and resource lifetime. No additional diagnostics or speculative synchronization change is justified by the current evidence, and no new CI run was dispatched for this investigation. Apple Silicon with forced Intel configuration does not reproduce the paravirtual device's execution behavior.
